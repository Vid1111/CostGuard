📋 Notes & Assumptions

  • Prices are on-demand estimates (compute only). Storage, IOPS, backups and Reserved Instance discounts are not included.
  • Savings are accurate even with estimate prices — a downsize keeps the same storage, so only compute cost changes.
  • Recommendations are same-family only (e.g. r5 → r5). Cross-family moves planned for V2.
  • Instances with fewer than 50% of expected CloudWatch datapoints over the 14-day window are skipped rather than incorrectly labelled idle.
  • Multi-AZ instances are billed at 2× the single-AZ price; savings are also doubled.
  • CPU uses the 95th percentile of hourly peaks to avoid rare spikes blocking a safe downgrade.
  • Memory uses the true peak (running out of RAM crashes a database; hot CPU only slows it).
